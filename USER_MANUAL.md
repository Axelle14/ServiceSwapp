## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. Click the **Sign Up** button on the homepage
2. Fill in the registration form:
   - **Full Name**: Your complete name
   - **Email**: A valid email address (used for login)

3. Click **Create Account**
4. You'll be logged in automatically and receive **50 starter credits** to begin trading

### Logging In

1. Click the **Login** button
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click **Sign In**
4. You'll be redirected to your dashboard

### Logging Out

- Click your profile icon in the top-right corner of the page
- Select **Logout**

## Understanding Credits

Credits are the virtual currency of SkillSwap. Here's what you need to know:

### Credit Basics

- **Starting Balance**: Every new user begins with 50 credits
- **Cost of Services**: Services have different credit costs depending on their complexity
- **No Money Involved**: Credits are earned by providing services and spent by requesting them
- **Fair Exchange**: The credit system ensures a balanced, fair marketplace

### Credit Flow

1. **Request a Service**: You spend credits (they are locked in escrow)
2. **Service Provider Accepts**: The swap begins
3. **Service is Completed**: You confirm the work is done
4. **Credits Released**: The service provider receives the credits
5. **Service Provider Provides Service**: They spend credits (locked in escrow)

### Viewing Your Credit Balance

Your current credit balance is displayed in the **top-right corner** of every page next to the credit icon (💰). You'll also see a detailed breakdown on your **Dashboard**.

### Browsing Services

#### Finding Services

1. Click **Browse Services** in the main navigation
2. You're now viewing all available services

#### Filtering and Searching

- **By Category**: Look for category filters (e.g., Design, Writing, Programming)
- **By Credit Cost**: Filter services within your credit budget
- **Search**: Use the search box to find specific skills or service names

#### Viewing Service Details

1. Click on any service card to view full details
2. You'll see:
   - **Service Title & Description**: What the service includes
   - **Credit Cost**: How many credits the service costs
   - **Provider Info**: Name and rating of the service provider
   - **Provider's Other Services**: See what else they offer
   - **Request This Service**: Button to start a swap

### Adding Your Services

#### Create a New Service

1. Click **Add Service** (usually available via a button on your profile or dashboard)
2. Fill in the service details:
   - **Service Title**: A clear, descriptive name (e.g., "Logo Design", "CV Writing", "Basic HTML Coding")
   - **Description**: Explain what you'll provide, how long it takes, any requirements
   - **Category**: Select the relevant category (e.g., Design, Writing, Tech)
   - **Credit Cost**: Set how many credits this service is worth (e.g., 15, 25, 40)

3. Click **Create Service**

#### Service Listing Guidelines

- Be **specific and clear** about what you're offering
- Include **realistic timeframes** (e.g., "I'll deliver within 3 business days")
- List **any requirements** from the person requesting the service
- Use honest **credit pricing** — compare similar services to price fairly

3. See all services you've listed

1. Find the service in your list

4. Click **Save Changes**

#### Delete a Service

1. Find the service in your list
2. Click the **Delete** button

## Creating and Managing Swaps

A "swap" is a transaction between two users. Here's how they work:

### Requesting a Service

#### Step 1: Find a Service

1. Go to **Browse Services**
2. Click on a service you want to request
3. Review the details and the provider's information

#### Step 2: Request the Service

1. On the service detail page, click **Request This Service**
2. You'll see a confirmation dialog:
   - Service title and cost
   - Provider's name
   - Your current credit balance (to confirm you have enough)

3. Click **Confirm** to proceed
4. **Your credits are now locked** — they'll be held in escrow until the swap completes

#### Step 3: Wait for Response

- The service provider will receive a notification
- They must accept or decline your request within a few days
- If they decline, your credits will be returned immediately
- You can see the status on your Dashboard

### Responding to Swap Requests

When someone requests your service, you'll receive a notification.

#### View Pending Requests

1. Go to your **Dashboard**
2. Look for "Pending Swap Requests" or "New Requests"
3. Click on a request to see details

#### Accept a Swap Request

1. Click on the swap request
2. Click **Accept** to agree to provide the service
3. The swap officially begins
4. You can now message the requester to discuss details

#### Decline a Swap Request

1. Click on the swap request
2. Click **Decline** to reject the service request
3. Their credits will be immediately returned
4. Optionally provide a reason (e.g., "I'm too busy", "Outside my expertise")

### Completing a Swap

#### For Service Providers

1. Once you've accepted a swap, **provide the service** to the requester
2. You can communicate via the built-in messaging system attached to the swap
3. Deliver the work (files, completed task, etc.) according to what was agreed
4. Mark the swap as **"Delivered"** in the system
5. Wait for the requester to confirm completion

#### For Service Requesters

1. Monitor the **Messages** section for your swap
2. Review the work delivered by the provider
3. If satisfied, click **Confirm Completion**
4. The service provider's credits are now released
5. You're prompted to leave a review

#### If There's a Problem

- **Use Messaging**: Talk to the other person through the built-in chat
- **Request Changes**: If the work isn't quite right, you can request revisions
- **Report a Dispute**: If you can't reach agreement, use the dispute system to escalate

### Reviewing Services

After a swap completes, you can review the service provider (and they can review you).

#### Leaving a Review

1. After confirming completion, you'll see a **"Leave a Review"** prompt
2. Provide:
   - **Star Rating**: 1-5 stars (5 = excellent, 1 = poor)
   - **Written Comment**: Share your experience (250 characters max)
   - **Recommend to Others**: Indicate if you'd work with them again

3. Click **Submit Review**

#### Professional Review Tips

- Be **honest and fair** in your ratings
- Provide **specific feedback** (e.g., what went well, what could improve)
- Be **respectful** — even if you're dissatisfied, avoid rude language
- Remember: Providers read these and use them to improve

#### Viewing Reviews

- Check a user's **average rating** on their profile
- Read **specific reviews** left by past clients
- Use review history to decide if you want to work with someone

## Messaging

SkillSwap has a built-in messaging system for communication during swaps.

### Accessing Messages

1. Click **Messages** in the main navigation
2. You'll see your message inbox with active conversations

### Starting a Conversation

1. When you create or accept a swap, a message thread automatically opens
2. Click the conversation to open the chat

### Sending Messages

1. Open a swap's message thread
2. Type your message in the text box at the bottom
3. Click **Send** or press Enter
4. Your message appears immediately

### Message Features

- **Real-time notifications**: You'll be notified of new messages
- **Message history**: All conversations are saved
- **Linked to swaps**: Each message thread is tied to a specific service swap

### Communication Best Practices

- **Be professional and courteous**
- **Respond within 24 hours** when possible
- **Ask clarifying questions** before starting work
- **Confirm deadlines and requirements**
- **Provide updates** on progress

## FAQ & Troubleshooting

### Account & Login

**Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?**
A: On the login page, look for a "Forgot Password?" link. Enter your email, and you'll receive reset instructions.

**Q: Can I change my email address?**
A: Yes. Go to your profile, click "Edit Profile," and update your email. Confirm the change via the link sent to your new email.

**Q: What if I see an error message during login?**
A: Check that your email is spelled correctly and your password is entered correctly. Passwords are case-sensitive. If you still can't log in, try resetting your password or contact support.

### Credits & Transactions

**Q: How many credits do I start with?**
A: Every new user receives 50 starter credits upon registration.

**Q: What happens if I don't have enough credits to request a service?**
A: You can't request the service until you've earned more credits by providing services to others.

**Q: Can I buy credits with real money?**
A: Currently, credits are earned only by providing services. No credit purchases are available. (Check back for future features.)

**Q: Can I get a refund of credits?**
A: Credits are used for trading services, not transactions that can be reversed. If a swap is declined, your credits are returned. If a swap is disputed, the dispute team will review.

**Q: My credits disappeared. What happened?**
A: Check your dashboard for recent swap activity. Your credits may be:
  - Locked in an active swap
  - Awaiting a provider's response
  - Recently transferred to another user

### Services

**Q: How much should I charge for my service?**
A: Look at similar services in the marketplace to price fairly. Complex or specialized services typically cost more (25-50 credits), while simple tasks cost less (10-20 credits).

**Q: Can I edit my service after listing it?**
A: Yes. Go to your profile, find the service, and click "Edit." You can change the title, description, category, and price.

**Q: What if I want to delete a service?**
A: You can delete services anytime from your profile. Pending swap requests won't be affected, but no new requests can be made.

**Q: Can the same swap request stay if I delete a service?**
A: No, the swap request is automatically canceled when you delete the associated service, and credits are returned to the requester.

### Swaps & Transactions

**Q: How long do I have to respond to a swap request?**
A: It's recommended to respond within 2-3 business days. Unreasonably long delays may affect your rating.

**Q: What if the service provider doesn't deliver on time?**
A: Use the messaging system to check on progress. Set clear deadlines before accepting. If there's a real problem, use the dispute system.

**Q: Can I cancel a swap I requested?**
A: Before the provider accepts, you can cancel and get your credits back. After they accept, you should discuss cancellation with them first.

**Q: What if I'm not satisfied with the service delivered?**
A: Message the provider to discuss concerns and request revisions. Most providers are happy to fix issues. If you can't reach agreement, submit a dispute.

### Reviews & Rating

**Q: Can I edit or delete a review I left?**
A: Reviews are permanent once submitted. Write carefully and be accurate with your rating.

**Q: What if I receive an unfair review?**
A: You can't delete it, but you can respond via message or request support review the case if it's clearly inappropriate or false.

**Q: How is my average rating calculated?**
A: It's the average of all star ratings you've received from completed swaps.

### Messaging

**Q: I didn't receive a message notification. What happened?**
A: Check your Messages inbox directly — some notifications may be delayed. Make sure notifications are enabled in your browser settings.

**Q: Can I message someone who isn't in a swap with me?**
A: The messaging system is currently linked to active swaps only. You can't message arbitrary users.

**Q: Can I unsend a message?**
A: No, messages are permanent once sent. Be careful with what you send.

### Subscriptions

**Q: What's the difference between Free, Premium, and Pro?**
A: Free is basic access. Premium adds advanced features and priority support. Pro includes unlimited listings and featured placement. See the Subscriptions page for full details.

**Q: Can I change my subscription plan later?**
A: Yes, upgrade or downgrade anytime. Changes take effect immediately.

**Q: Is my subscription auto-renewing?**
A: Check your subscription settings. You can turn off auto-renewal or cancel anytime without penalty.

### General Issues

**Q: I received a 404 or 500 error. What should I do?**
A: This indicates a technical problem. Try:
  1. Refresh the page
  2. Clear your browser cache
  3. Try a different browser
  4. Report the error with screenshots if it persists

**Q: The page is loading slowly. What can I do?**
A: Try:
  1. Refresh the page
  2. Check your internet connection
  3. Try again in a different browser
  4. Check back later if the server is having issues

**Q: Is my data secure? How is my password stored?**
A: SkillSwap uses industry-standard security:
  - Passwords are hashed with bcrypt (never stored in plain text)
  - All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS)
  - Personal information is never shared with third parties
